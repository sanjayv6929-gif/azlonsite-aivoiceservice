    FAQs

    Questions you might have

    Is there a monthly fee?+

    No. You pay the one-time setup, then ₹5 per minute of calls. Your first 15 minutes are free, and you recharge from ₹100.

    How does calling faster double conversion?+

    A lead is most interested in the first few minutes after they enquire. Most teams call back hours later, after the lead has spoken to a competitor or lost interest. The agent calls within seconds, every time, including nights and weekends.

    What's the difference between inbound and outbound?+

    An inbound agent answers calls coming to your number. An outbound agent calls your new leads, for example the moment someone fills in your Meta ad form.

    What do you need from me to start?+

    Your call script and access to the tools you want connected. The delivery timeline starts once we have these.

    Which languages can the agent speak?+

    We set it up in your customers' main language, including Tamil, Hindi and English. Add more languages with the multilingual voice add-on for ₹499 each.

    How do I know the leads are qualified?+

    We write your qualifying questions into the script together: budget, timeline, location, need. The agent only books meetings with leads who pass, and every call is logged so you can review it.

    Ready to call every lead in seconds?

    A quick 30-minute call, no pressure. We'll look at your leads and show you what an agent would do for them. If it's not a fit, we'll tell you.
